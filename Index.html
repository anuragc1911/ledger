A Gmail search. Add your bank's sender to make it stricter, e.g. from:(alerts@hdfcbank.net OR credit_cards@icicibank.com). Categories come from the Rules tab in your sheet.

SMS: Google can't read texts on your phone. Most banks send the same alert by email. If yours only texts, an SMS-to-Gmail forwarding app (Android) or a Shortcuts automation (iPhone) can send them to your inbox, and they'll be picked up here.
