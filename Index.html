Where the benchmarks come from

Indian bank and fintech guidance: Kotak, Bank of Baroda, Axis Bank, Bajaj Finserv, Policybazaar, Acko, Finnovate and freefincal. Global references: Fidelity's savings-by-age multiples, and SoFi and DBS on personal financial ratios. Sources often disagree, so every target here is a rule of thumb, not a rule.
